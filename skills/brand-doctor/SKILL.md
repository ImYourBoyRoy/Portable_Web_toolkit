```bash

# Typical: audit / repair against --site-profile (see module README)
```

## Rules

1. Follow client `BRAND_GUIDE.md` over generic defaults.
2. No stock/placeholder favicons or heroes.
3. Dry-run or show plan before destructive asset overwrites.
